TASBIH ONLINE

Tasbih Online — guide and help

A free dhikr counter for your phone and computer. No account required.

Using the counter

  1. Tap the large counting area. Each tap adds one.
  2. Choose a dhikr collection or a free counter under Choose dhikr.
  3. Focus at the top shows only the counter. × returns to the usual screen.
  4. Theme switches between light and dark.
  5. In Knowledge, choose a book and use its buttons or swipe across a page.

Add to your phone

On Android, use the Chrome menu to add the site to your home screen or install it. On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Menu wording depends on your device language.

About Tasbih Online

Your count stays in this browser. There are no accounts or device synchronization. Book pages are short reflections on the meaning of the linked sources. Check each source for its original wording.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the counter?

Tap the circle once for each dhikr. Free mode has no target. The reset button clears the current count.

How does 33–33–33–1 work?

Choose After prayer. The app moves through Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu akbar 33 times each, then the full final tahlil once. Source: Sahih Muslim 597a.

What happens at 100?

The round stops when complete. Choose Another round to start again.

How do I show only the circle?

Tap the round Focus button at the top. Tap × to return.

Is my count saved?

Automatically in this browser, when local storage is available. Clearing browser data removes it. Counts do not sync across devices.

Does it work offline?

After a complete first visit over HTTPS, the main app is cached. Quran audio and source links need internet access.

Can I put Tasbih Online on my home screen?

Use your browser menu. In Safari on iPhone choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Why is there no sound or vibration?

Check Tasbih Online settings and device volume. Sound starts after interaction. Vibration depends on browser and device support.

How do I reveal Sunnah?

Open the Sunnah tab. The reminder is already visible. Next opens another reminder.

Are these exact hadith quotations?

The cards contain brief paraphrases and practical reminders, with source links. Consult a qualified scholar for religious rulings.

How do I clear all data?

Open settings, choose Clear local app data and confirm. Only this browser’s Tasbih Online data is cleared.

How do I turn book pages?

Choose a book in Knowledge. Use Back and Next or swipe horizontally on the page. Sounds and reduced motion follow your settings.
